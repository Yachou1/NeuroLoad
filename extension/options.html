Automatic mode and allowed sites

One initial Chrome grant for automatic mode. This gives technically broad access to HTTPS sites. NeuroLoad then asks for consent before analyzing recognized sensitive sites, including government services. These conservative rules are imperfect: an unrecognized sensitive site may activate automatically.

Sensitive-site consent lasts 15 minutes for the displayed site and tab. Recognized login, payment, private pages and forms remain blocked. You can decline or revoke consent from the extension icon. Sensitive profiles are temporary and are not written to local disk storage by NeuroLoad.
